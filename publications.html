Thesis

PhD thesis2026
Helping integrate privacy concerns into software: a model-driven engineering framework for GDPR-compliant software development
Joint PhD, Université de Montpellier (France) & Université de Blida 1 (Algeria). Defence: October 2026.

As software becomes ever more personalised, the collection and exploitation of personal data has reached unprecedented levels. Despite legal obligations such as the GDPR, there is no universally accepted method to integrate privacy management into applications. The thesis introduces a tool-supported, actionable approach built on the PRIAM metamodel (Privacy Assessment Method), which models the GDPR and serves as the basis for generic user stories, a database schema and a domain-specific language. A non-intrusive access-control and consent-management protocol, based on ABAC and the sidecar architecture pattern, minimises the impact on the provider's application.
