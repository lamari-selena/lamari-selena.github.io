About

I am a researcher and lecturer in software engineering. My work focuses on helping developers build software that respects users' privacy by design, with a particular interest in compliance with the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

My doctoral research, carried out under a joint supervision agreement between the Université de Montpellier (France) and the Université de Blida 1 (Algeria), proposes a model-driven, tool-supported framework to integrate privacy concerns into software, centred on the PRIAM metamodel for GDPR. Beyond privacy engineering, I work on secure software development: security of LLM-generated code, Security by Design and DevSecOps training, and the privacy impact of security tooling.

I currently teach software engineering, object-oriented modelling, programming and databases at ENSIBS (cybersecurity and software systems engineering school) and its partner IUT in Vannes, and I supervise student projects and internships. Before that I taught at the Université de Montpellier and the Université de Blida 1.
